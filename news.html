New Mombasa Branch Launched

Biashara Community Sacco proudly opened its third branch in Mombasa on July 10, 2025. The new branch aims to provide accessible financial services, including savings accounts, loans, and financial literacy programs, to the coastal community. The opening ceremony was attended by local leaders and over 200 members, marking a significant milestone in our mission to empower businesses across Kenya.

Financial Literacy Drive Success
June 15, 2025

Over 300 members attended our financial literacy workshop in Nairobi.

Financial Literacy Drive Success

Our financial literacy workshop, held on June 15, 2025, in Nairobi, drew over 300 participants. The event focused on budgeting, investment strategies, and loan management, empowering members to make informed financial decisions. Expert speakers from the banking sector shared insights, and interactive sessions provided practical tools for financial planning.

2025 AGM Highlights
May 5, 2025

Annual General Meeting sets new goals for 2025.

2025 AGM Highlights

The 2025 Annual General Meeting, held on May 5, 2025, brought together over 500 members to review 2024 performance and set ambitious goals for 2025. Key highlights included a 20% increase in membership and the approval of new loan products tailored for small businesses. The meeting also elected new board members to steer the Sacco forward.

Community Outreach in Eldoret

Scheduled for August 20, 2025, our community outreach in Eldoret will focus on financial inclusion for underserved communities. The program includes free financial literacy workshops, business consultations, and networking opportunities. We aim to empower local entrepreneurs with the tools to grow their businesses sustainably.
