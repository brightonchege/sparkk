About Us

Birth and Registration

Biashara Community Sacco was established as a community-based Sacco in Ruiru sub-county and its environs by shareholders of Neo-Horizon Holding Ltd, a company of born-again Christians. It was officially registered on 20th May 2010 with the Commissioner for Cooperative Development under certificate No. CS/12574 and commenced operations on 1st June 2010.

Mission

The mission of Biashara Community Sacco is to empower economically active people within its areas of operation, with particular emphasis on those running small and medium-scale businesses.

Motto

Boresha Maisha (Making life better)

Objectives of Biashara Community Sacco

  • Encourage thrift among community members living in Ruiru sub-county and its environs by affording them an opportunity for accumulating savings.
  • Create a source of funds for credit, which shall be lent to members at fair and reasonable rates of interest.
  • Provide an opportunity for each member to grow their business, thereby improving their respective economic and social conditions.
