Our Loan Products

Beba Loan

  • Target: Associate members
  • Features: Asset finance for land, cars, motorbikes
  • Requirement: Save 40-50% of property value
  • Facility: 50-60% of asset value
  • Interest: 12% p.a.
  • Repayment: 6-36 months
  • Waiting Period: 3-7 days

Masomo Loan

  • Target: Members with Masomo account
  • Features: Financing for school fees
  • Requirement: School fees pro-forma or admission letter
  • Facility: Up to 5x savings or Kshs. 100,000
  • Interest: 10.5% p.a.
  • Repayment: 3-12 months
  • Waiting Period: 1-3 days

Mortgage Loan

  • Target: Full members with mortgage account
  • Requirement: Approved building plans, 20% savings
  • Facility: Up to Kshs. 12 million, 3-6 months grace period
  • Interest: 12% p.a.
  • Repayment: 6-60 months
  • Waiting Period: 3-7 days

InstantPesa Loan

New!
  • Target: All members
  • Features: Quick cash for emergencies
  • Requirement: Active savings account
  • Facility: Up to Kshs. 50,000
  • Interest: 8% p.a.
  • Repayment: 1-6 months
  • Waiting Period: Same day
